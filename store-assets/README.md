## Description
Billory is a free, offline invoice maker and billing app built for freelancers who invoice clients abroad. Create professional invoices and estimates, record partial payments, and see who owes you at a glance — no account, no subscription, nothing leaves your computer.

- Money-owed dashboard: outstanding, overdue, and paid this month in your home currency
- Multi-currency invoicing: invoice in your client's currency with manual exchange rates
- Partial payments: record every payment with date, method, and reference
- 8 invoice templates plus a statement of account with running balance
- Quotes convert to invoices in one click; recurring invoices prepare drafts automatically
- Tax: line or invoice level, multiple named rates, inclusive/exclusive, withholding deductions
- Time tracking: roll unbilled hours straight into an invoice
- Payment reminder drafts (polite / firm / final) — you send them, the app never does
- PDF export, CSV export, JSON backup & import — your data stays yours
- Gap-free invoice numbering and automatic history snapshots so you never lose data

Fully offline: no cloud, no sign-in, no paywall on the export button.
